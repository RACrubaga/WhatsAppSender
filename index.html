4. Send

Send message opens WhatsApp with the text ready. Add a flyer to get a Copy flyer button per contact.

search
help How sending works
chatText message

Click Send message. WhatsApp opens with the personalised text typed in. Press Send in WhatsApp.

imageWith a flyer (computer)

Click Copy flyer, then Send message. In the chat press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac). The flyer attaches and your text becomes its caption.

smartphoneWith a flyer (phone)

Tap Share on a contact, choose WhatsApp, then pick the contact. The message is also copied so you can paste it as the caption.

shieldStay safe

Send at a steady pace. Messaging many people who haven't saved your number in a short time can get a number restricted by WhatsApp.
