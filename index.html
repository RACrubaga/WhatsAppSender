How sending works
  • Send opens WhatsApp with the message already typed. Press Send inside WhatsApp.
  • With a flyer on a computer: click Copy flyer & open chat, then press Ctrl+V (Cmd+V on Mac) in the chat. The flyer attaches and the message becomes its caption.
  • With a flyer on a phone: tap Share, pick WhatsApp, then choose the contact.
  • Send at a steady pace. Messaging many people who haven't saved your number in a short time can get a number restricted by WhatsApp.
